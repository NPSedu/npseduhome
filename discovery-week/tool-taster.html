STEP 3 YOUR MINI BUILD CHALLENGE 10–12 mins
🎯 CHALLENGE

Build one complete room or area of an adventure world. It needs: an NPC who tells the player their goal, one challenge or puzzle to solve, and a clear way out when they're done.

  • 💡Keep it small — one room with one challenge is a complete game zone
  • 💡Use signs on walls for extra instructions — keep the text short and clear
  • 💡A locked door + a hidden key = a simple but satisfying puzzle
  • 💡Test in Adventure mode before you're done — it always feels different as a player

STEP 3 YOUR MINI BUILD CHALLENGE 10–12 mins
🎯 CHALLENGE

Build a small interactive 3D scene with at least two objects that do something when you tap or click them, and a clear goal for the player — collect something, find something, or hear a story.

  • 💡Keep it to one room — two interactive objects done well beats a huge empty world
  • 💡Use "When clicked → Say" to give characters dialogue — it makes the world feel alive instantly
  • 💡Use "When clicked → Hide" to make a collectible disappear when the player finds it
  • 💡Test often — tap Play after every change to see if it works the way you expect
