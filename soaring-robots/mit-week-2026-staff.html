Key Reminders

The quick-reference version

Don't forget

  • STEAM Tokens — 2 per team from Monday. They justify the need to you; no token, no resource.
  • Award MIT Points via ePraise — it's your job to give them out, not just to watch for them. Award visibly, say why. See the Giving MIT Points guide above, or ask Sam if you need a refresher on logging it.
  • This is still teaching — bring your usual toolkit: Challenge, Explanation, Modelling, Practice & Feedback, Oracy.
  • Pace is flexible, readiness isn't — the only fixed point is every team ready for Friday's Expo, 10:20–11:40, Sports Hall.
  • Tidy space = MIT Points — a messy stall or base room can cost eligibility.
