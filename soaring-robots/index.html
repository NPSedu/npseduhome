The Week

Your week, stage by stage

Tap a stage below to see what you'll be doing, and when your next checkpoint is.

Monday · 3:20 · Checkpoint 1

What problem and possibilities are we exploring?

Launch Assembly, the Great MIT Supply Drop, Team Roles, and your first look at the challenge.

Tuesday · 1:20 · Checkpoint 2

What have we learned from nature?

Research the animal you're taking inspiration from, and what makes its abilities special.

Tuesday · 3:20 · Checkpoint 3

What are we going to build?

Decide what you're building, and how it will mimic your chosen animal's anatomy.

Wednesday · 3:20 · Checkpoint 4

What have we created so far?

Guest speaker assembly, then Create begins — this is when your STEAM Tokens come into play.

Thursday · 12:20 · Checkpoint 5

What did we test, learn and improve?

Test your build, use the Ladder of Feedback, and improve it before your next checkpoint.

Thursday · 3:20 · Checkpoint 6

Are we ready to tell our story?

Reflect on what you've learned, and get your stall and story ready to share.

Friday · 10:20–11:40 · The Expo

Share what you've built.

Set up your stall and present to visitors — followed by the Final Celebration and the House MIT Trophy.

Key Info

The things worth knowing before you start

All of this is covered in more depth in your Journal and Workbook — this is the quick-reference version.

2

STEAM Tokens

Every team gets 2 tokens on Monday. Take a token to a specialist, explain what you need and why, and they'll help you out. No token, no resource. Need more? Ask your facilitator and justify it.

★

MIT Points

You're collecting MIT Points all week. Show any of the 6Cs — Communication, Collaboration, Creativity, Critical Thinking, Citizenship, Character — and a teacher can award your House an MIT Point on ePraise. Most points by Friday wins the House MIT Trophy.

9

Team Roles

Every team member leads something — Project Manager, Biology Lead, Engineering Lead, Design Lead, Prototype Lead, Innovation Lead, Communications Lead, Videographer, Expo Stall Manager.

6

Checkpoints

Six points across the week where you check in with your facilitator. They're there to help you pace yourself — going faster or slower than another team is completely fine.

AI

Using AI This Week

Different stages allow different levels of AI help — from none at Define, to more at Learn & Explore. Your facilitator will talk you through what's okay at each stage.

📋

Three Documents

Your Individual Innovation Journal (personal), your Team Design Workbook (shared, becomes your Expo portfolio), and your A1 Team Poster (the live visual wall of your progress).

House MIT Points

Which House is winning?

Every time a teacher spots one of the 6Cs — Communication, Collaboration, Creativity, Critical Thinking, Citizenship, Character — they can award your House an MIT Point on ePraise. Points add up across the whole week; the House with the most on Friday wins the House MIT Trophy.
